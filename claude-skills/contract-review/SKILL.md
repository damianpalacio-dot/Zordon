# Contract review — GEC2 as electrical subcontractor

This is a review aid, not legal advice. Say that at the top of the memo, and flag anything serious for counsel or management.

## Read

The whole agreement, including exhibits (scope, schedule, insurance, safety, rates, flow-down of the prime contract), plus GEC2's proposal or estimate if provided. Quote section numbers for everything you cite.

## Check every item and rate it High / Medium / Low

- **Scope:** matches GEC2's proposal? Hidden scope (e.g. low voltage, fire alarm, temp power, trenching, concrete pads, utility fees, permits, testing, commissioning, BIM, prefab, as-builts). Note the exclusions and qualifications that got dropped.
- **Schedule:** liquidated damages and pass-through, acceleration without compensation, no-damage-for-delay, milestone dates vs gear lead times (switchgear, transformers, generators).
- **Payment:** pay-if-paid vs pay-when-paid; retention % and when it's released; billing deadlines; SOV and AIA requirements; stored materials (long-lead gear) billable?
- **Changes:** notice deadlines (days), pricing deadlines, markup caps, T&M rates and sign-off rules, directives to proceed, claims process.
- **Risk transfer:** indemnity (breadth, defend vs indemnify), additional insured endorsements, waiver of subrogation, consequential damages waiver (mutual?), limitation of liability.
- **Insurance and bonds:** limits vs GEC2's current program; bond requirements and who pays.
- **Back-charges, clean-up, safety:** back-charge notice and right to cure; safety program flow-downs.
- **Warranty:** start date (substantial completion vs owner acceptance), length, and the manufacturer warranty pass-through.
- **Termination and disputes:** termination for convenience (payment for work and stored materials), venue, arbitration, attorney fees.
- **Public / labor:** prevailing wage, DIR, PLA, LAUSD / airport requirements, certified payroll, DBE/MBE reporting.

## Output

A Word memo (or a doc, if Damian prefers) with:

1. A summary (5 bullets: biggest risks and what to push back on).
2. A table: section | what it says | risk | recommended redline / ask | owner.
3. Missing items: exclusions and qualifications to add, and a questions list for the GC.

Save it to the job's `01 COST CONTROL` per `Zordon/FILING.md`, e.g. `G2707_BURB RPT - CONTRACT - Subcontract Review Rosendin (10.05.2026).docx`.
